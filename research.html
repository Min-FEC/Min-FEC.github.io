Research Highlights

Coexistence of Heterogeneous Communication Services

Modern wireless systems must support heterogeneous services such as eMBB and URLLC. These services can have different blocklength and error-probability constraints, making purely orthogonal resource allocation difficult as the number of devices and services grows.

This research direction designs transmission schemes based on practical channel coding and modulation to support heterogeneous services effectively and simultaneously. A central feature is single-user-based encoding and decoding for each user.

Spatially Coupled Codes and Modern Channel Coding

This line of work studies coding structures and iterative decoding methods for reliable high-throughput communications, including spatially coupled LDPC codes, turbo-like codes, product-like codes, and related code constructions.

Delay-Doppler Domain Communications and ISAC

This line of work studies orthogonal delay-Doppler division multiplexing, integrated sensing and communication waveforms, and receiver algorithms for communication and sensing systems.
